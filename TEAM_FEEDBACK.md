# Sharp Photocopier Configurator - Team Feedback

## Purpose

This configurator helps users select a Sharp photocopier model, choose compatible accessories and consumables, validate the configuration, and download a PDF summary.

Please review the app and share feedback on usability, accuracy, missing product data, and anything that feels confusing or incorrect.

## What To Test

1. Select different Sharp models from the dropdown.
2. Review whether the listed accessories match the selected model.
3. Select accessories such as paper drawers, finishers, punch modules, folding units, and consumables.
4. Check whether dependency warnings make sense.
5. Confirm that incompatible options are disabled correctly.
6. Complete a valid configuration and confirm the summary looks correct.
7. Download the PDF and review the exported information.
